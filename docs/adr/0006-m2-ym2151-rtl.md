# ADR-0006: Select JT51 for the M2 local YM2151 proof

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-01
- Deciders: RPCMP maintainers

## Context

M2 needs one YM2151-compatible RTL implementation to prove the fixed synthetic
sequence, the Core-to-RTL queue, and Pocket's 48 kHz AUDIO boundary. This choice
must be reproducible and must not imply that the unresolved Pocket execution
substrate or a redistributable release has been approved.

The target spike synthesized JT51 revision
`985a573dcfc1ff135553a39f7eae21d18ba57cbe` standalone and in a stripped
openfpgaOS research build. The integrated 90 MHz fit used 13,774 ALMs, 172 of
308 M10Ks, 13 DSPs, and two PLLs, with worst reported setup slack `+0.327 ns`
and hold slack `+0.110 ns`. Its queue/JT51 simulation passed, but JT51 audio was
not connected to Pocket AUDIO and inherited APF external-I/O paths remained
unconstrained. These measurements establish bounded feasibility, not M2 audio
acceptance.

JT51 is GPL-3.0-or-later. RPCMP currently has no repository-level license,
NOTICE, REUSE manifest, or approved process for conveying a GPL-covered
bitstream with complete corresponding source and build material.

## Decision

- Use JT51 revision `985a573dcfc1ff135553a39f7eae21d18ba57cbe` as the
  YM2151-compatible RTL for M2 local development and hardware verification.
- Consume it from a revision-checked research checkout. Do not vendor source,
  generated HDL, or a JT51-derived bitstream into this repository under this
  ADR.
- Treat its input clock, clock enables, reset, sample cadence, signed sample
  interpretation, and register handshake as adapter-owned boundaries. The
  adapter must be specified and self-tested before Pocket integration.
- Keep the project-owned `DeviceOp`, scheduler, and Core-to-RTL queue contracts
  independent of JT51. Replacing the sound implementation must not change those
  contracts.
- Do not publish or otherwise convey a JT51-derived RPCMP bitstream or package
  until a later accepted decision supplies the GPLv3 section 6 compliance
  process: exact corresponding source, all local modifications, generator and
  build inputs, reproducible commands or scripts, license and copyright
  notices, and the chosen source-offer/conveyance method.
- Do not treat this decision as selecting openfpgaOS. ADR-0004 remains accepted
  and unsuperseded for the execution substrate.

## Exact development inputs

- JT51 source: `jotego/jt51` commit
  `985a573dcfc1ff135553a39f7eae21d18ba57cbe`.
- License evidence: the pinned repository's GPL-3.0 license text and
  GPL-3.0-or-later HDL headers.
- FPGA target: Cyclone V `5CEBA4F23C8`.
- Synthesis: Quartus Prime Lite 25.1std.0 Build 1129.
- Simulation: Questa Altera Starter FPGA Edition 2025.2.
- Prior research integration inputs and artifact hashes remain recorded in
  `docs/design/pocket-openfpgaos-spike.md`; they are not production inputs.

## Consequences

M2 can now implement and simulate one concrete 48 kHz audio adapter without
making the sound RTL swappable boundary ambiguous. Local, checksum-pinned
Pocket experiments may use JT51, but their artifacts remain non-distributable.
Slice 4 is only partially complete: a superseding execution-substrate ADR and
an approved corresponding-source/distribution process are still required
before the integrated Pocket build can be described as releasable.

## Alternatives considered

- **Delay all sound-adapter work until the substrate decision:** avoids a
  provisional integration, but needlessly blocks a platform-owned and
  simulation-testable boundary for which resource feasibility is already
  measured.
- **Write a new YM2151 implementation:** would greatly expand M2 and add a
  fidelity-validation project unrelated to proving transport and Pocket audio.
- **Select an unpinned alternative core:** would not provide reproducible
  source, license, resource, or timing evidence.
